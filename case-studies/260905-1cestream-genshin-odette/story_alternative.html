Alternative Story

Story Overview

Recommended Title

I Finally Figured Out Who Was Controlling Inazuma

Alternative Titles

  1. The Fatui Were Behind Everything in Inazuma
  2. I Stopped Caring About My Sister—This Conspiracy Is Bigger

Description

While advancing Inazuma’s resistance story, the streamer grows suspicious of a mysterious sponsor, witnesses the human cost of Fatui Delusions, and discovers evidence that the Fatui are prolonging the war. His interest shifts away from the Traveler’s missing sister toward the Fatui conspiracy, culminating in the revelation that Raiden Shogun is a puppet and confirmation that corrupt commissions helped manipulate the Vision Hunt Decree.

Why This Story

This is a meaningfully different, lore-driven story with escalating suspicions, direct consequences, a compelling antagonist confrontation, a major puppet revelation, and a strong confirmation payoff. It ends honestly at the point where the conspiracy is substantially exposed but the final confrontation and complete answers remain for a later stream.

Story Arc

Premise

Joining Inazuma’s resistance exposes the streamer to a suspicious anonymous sponsor and unexplained political decisions surrounding the Vision Hunt Decree.

Goal

Understand who is benefiting from the war and how the Vision Hunt Decree was imposed.

Obstacle

The sponsor’s identity, the Fatui’s method of influence, and the distinction between Raiden Shogun and her puppet remain hidden or only partially explained.

Payoff

The quest confirms that the Fatui cultivated demand for Delusions and colluded with commissions to exploit the puppet’s pursuit of eternity, validating the streamer’s broader conspiracy theory while leaving the final resolution unfinished.

Intro

Purpose

Establish that the Fatui conspiracy has become more compelling to the streamer than the game’s stated missing-sister objective.

Hook

After encountering the Fatui in every region and behind every crisis, he declares that they feel like the real main characters of Genshin.

Viewer Question

How deeply have the Fatui infiltrated Inazuma, and are they responsible for the Vision Hunt Decree?

Conclusion

Purpose

Pay off the streamer’s suspicions with the supported political explanation while ending before the still-unseen final confrontation.

Resolution

The quest confirms that the Fatui exploited the puppet’s single-minded pursuit of eternity and colluded with the Tenryo and Kanjou Commissions to create demand for Delusions and keep the conflict alive. The plan to expose that betrayal begins, but the full defeat of the conspiracy remains unresolved.

Final Takeaway

The Fatui did not need to overpower Inazuma directly; they manipulated its institutions, supplied the conflict, and used the Shogun puppet’s rigid programming against the nation.
