HOW IT WORKS

How ShadyCut turns
streams into first cuts.

01 — UNDERSTAND THE SESSION

We make sense of everything.

ShadyCut first works out what is actually happening across the entire session. It listens to what the streamer says, tracks who is speaking, follows gameplay activity and progression, notices reactions and face-cam activity, and combines important audio and visual signals with what is happening on screen.

That means understanding the people, the gameplay, and the context around each moment — including items, locations, characters, objectives, and events — so later decisions are based on what the moment actually means, not just how loud or flashy it looks.

Yes, it can tell when you start swearing> too.

GAME AWARENESS

The same moment means different things in different games.

ShadyCut uses game-specific knowledge to understand terminology, mechanics, locations, items, characters, progression and events. This helps it interpret what the streamer is talking about and what the viewer is actually seeing.

  • “TV Station” → an Arena Breakout map
  • “Character Pull” → a Genshin character acquisition
  • “Launch Site Key” → a Rust monument access item
  • “Knock” → downing another player

02 — FIND THE STORY

We figure out what the session is really about.

ShadyCut stops thinking in isolated clips and reconstructs the bigger picture. It looks for the goal, the obstacles, the decisions, the changes in momentum, and the payoff that turn a long session into something worth watching.

Instead of treating every loud or flashy moment as equally important, it works out what the viewer actually needs to see for the story to make sense — what sets things up, what changes the direction, and what pays off later.

03 — DIRECT THE EDIT

We shape the story for YouTube.

Once the story is understood, ShadyCut turns it into an actual edit plan. It selects the scenes that matter, decides what should come before or after them, trims unnecessary material, fills missing context, and checks whether the story still makes sense after the cuts.

  • Scene selection
  • Scene order
  • Recommended trims
  • Missing setup or payoff checks
  • Continuity checks
  • Pacing guidance
  • MUST KEEP and REVIEW guidance

BUILT FOR YOUTUBE

Story first. Retention always in mind.

“Optimized for YouTube” does not mean cutting everything as short as possible. It means protecting the story while constantly asking where attention might drop, where context arrives too late, and where pacing can be tightened without damaging the payoff. The goal is not maximum cuts — it is removing everything the viewer does not need to mentally survive.

  • Strong opening without giving away the whole story
  • Context before confusion
  • Remove repetition and dead space
  • Preserve setup → escalation → payoff
  • Tighten pacing without turning the edit into TikTok

04 — EDITABLE TIMELINE

You get a first cut, ready to make your own.

ShadyCut does not hand you a locked AI-generated video. It gives you a structured editable first cut that a creator or editor can open, review, tighten, restyle and finish.

  • Premiere-ready editable timeline
  • Selected source clips
  • Scene notes and context
  • Editorial guidance
  • Story Blueprint
  • Easy to review and change
  • Built to save time, not replace the editor
