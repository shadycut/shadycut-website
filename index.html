Questions streamers frequently ask.

How does the private beta selection work?

Anyone can apply through the beta form. I manually review every submission and select projects that best help improve ShadyCut. During the private beta, the focus is on testing different games, creator styles, and real-world workflows—not on first come, first served.

Which editors are supported?

Premiere Pro is currently supported through XML export. Support for additional editors is planned.

Do you edit my videos?

No. ShadyCut prepares the story, structure and editing plan. The creative editing remains yours.

How long does processing take?

Most streams are processed in around 15–20 minutes, depending on length and complexity.

Which games are supported?

The first game-specific profile was built for ARC Raiders, but ShadyCut also works without game-specific knowledge. As development continues, I'll keep adding dedicated game profiles to improve accuracy and storytelling for more titles.

Can I use ShadyCut for any platform?

Yes. ShadyCut creates a structured editing draft that you can turn into content for YouTube, TikTok, Shorts, Reels, or any other platform. Your final edit is yours to publish wherever you want.

Will AI replace my editing?

No. ShadyCut removes the repetitive preparation work. Your editing style, pacing and creative decisions remain your own.
