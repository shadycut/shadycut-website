CASE STUDY

From a two-hour stream to one story worth watching.

For Chowds, ShadyCut found a complete solo Arena Breakout arc across three raids and turned it into a structured 7.5-minute editing draft.

Date
August 2, 2026
Game
Arena Breakout
Streamer
Chowds

The source was a two-hour Arena Breakout stream. Instead of treating it as a collection of disconnected highlights, ShadyCut reviewed the session as a whole and selected three raids that could carry a single, coherent story.

The story opens with a fast one-tap and a high-value extract, then turns when Chowds refuses a knife-fight truce and loses a tense four-versus-one fight. He owns the mistake, resets with a clearer loadout plan, and closes the session with a 10-kill, two-million-value redemption run.

What ShadyCut prepared

  • A Story Blueprint connecting the strongest moments across three raids.
  • A Director Plan covering scene order, trims, transitions, pacing, and dialogue candidates.
  • A 7.5-minute structured video draft, ready for the creator or editor to shape.
  • The final story and edit plan in approximately 15–20 minutes of processing.

The first review found a smooth, coherent flow, strong key-moment selection, and useful planning files. It also surfaced practical improvements: some cuts were too aggressive, while automatic music and captions would add value in a future pass.

This is the difference between collecting highlights and finding a story. The final draft keeps the decision, setback, adjustment, and payoff connected, giving the editor a narrative starting point instead of another timeline to search from scratch.
