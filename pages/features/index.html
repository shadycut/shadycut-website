FEATURES

Everything ShadyCut
brings to the edit.

ShadyCut combines session understanding, game context, story reasoning, editorial guidance and an editable first cut — all built around long-form gaming content.

01 — BUILT DIFFERENT

Story-first by design.

STORY-FIRST EDITING

Looks for the story behind the footage instead of simply collecting highlight moments.

BUILT FOR LONG SESSIONS

Designed around hours of footage, not just short clips or isolated moments.

CREATIVE CONTROL

ShadyCut handles the editorial groundwork while the creator stays in control of the final edit.

02 — UNDERSTAND THE SESSION

Know what actually happened.

MULTI-SIGNAL UNDERSTANDING

Speech, gameplay, visual activity and audio context are understood together.

SPEAKER AWARENESS

Keeps track of who is speaking and connects dialogue with what is happening around it.

SESSION-WIDE CONTEXT

Moments are understood in the context of the full recording, not in isolation.

TRANSCRIPT & DIALOGUE

Speech is structured into useful, time-aware context for the session.

VISUAL & AUDIO CONTEXT

Important visual changes and audio cues help explain what is happening.

TIMESTAMPED EVENTS

Relevant session activity can be anchored to specific moments in the recording.

SESSION UNDERSTANDING

More than a transcript. More than gameplay footage.

ShadyCut connects speech, gameplay, events and context across the full session so later story decisions are based on what the moment actually means.

03 — GAME INTELLIGENCE

Understand the game too.

GAME-AWARE CONTEXT

Supported games are understood in their own terminology, objectives, locations, items and events.

GAMEPLAY PROGRESSION

Follows how the session develops so meaningful changes are not treated as isolated moments.

MEANING, NOT JUST ACTION

A quiet discovery can matter more than an explosion. Context helps determine why a moment matters.

04 — FIND THE STORY

Find what is worth telling.

NARRATIVE STRUCTURE

Connects goals, problems, decisions, setbacks, turning points and payoffs into a coherent story.

STORY-RELEVANT MOMENTS

Separates moments that move the story forward from footage that merely looks exciting.

SETUP → PAYOFF

Keeps earlier context connected to later consequences, callbacks and resolutions.

TURNING POINTS

Recognizes the moments where the direction, stakes or meaning of the session changes.

SUPPORTING CONTEXT

Keeps the context needed to make important moments understandable and satisfying.

MULTIPLE STORY OPPORTUNITIES

A long session can contain more than one story worth turning into a video.

FROM SESSION TO STORY

See the story take shape.

The full session becomes a structured story, then a first cut that is ready for a human editor to refine.

05 — SHAPE THE EDIT

Turn the story into a cut.

SCENE SELECTION

Identifies which scenes belong in the story and which ones do not.

SCENE ORDER

Organizes scenes into a sequence that keeps the story clear and progressing.

STORY INTEGRITY CHECKS

Flags continuity problems, incomplete setups and missing payoffs before they become confusing edits.

PACING & TRIM GUIDANCE

Highlights repetition, dead space and material that can be tightened without damaging the story.

MISSING CONTEXT

Surfaces places where the viewer may need setup or explanation before a moment can land.

MUST KEEP & REVIEW

Separates critical story material from moments that deserve a human editorial decision.

06 — EDITABLE FIRST CUT

Ready for the human finish.

EDITABLE TIMELINE

Selected footage arrives organized into a structured first cut that can be reviewed and changed.

EDITORIAL CONTEXT INCLUDED

Scene context and editorial guidance stay attached so important reasoning is not lost at handoff.

READY TO REFINE

Move scenes, change trims, restyle the edit or take it in another direction. The first cut is a starting point, not a locked result.
