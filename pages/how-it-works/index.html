HOW IT WORKS

How ShadyCut turns
streams into first cuts.

01 — UNDERSTAND THE SESSION

We make sense of everything.

ShadyCut first works out what is actually happening across the entire session. It listens to what the streamer says, tracks who is speaking, follows gameplay activity and progression, notices reactions and face-cam activity, and combines important audio and visual signals with what is happening on screen.

That means understanding the people, the gameplay, and the context around each moment — including items, locations, characters, objectives, and events — so later decisions are based on what the moment actually means, not just how loud or flashy it looks.

Yes, it can tell when you start swearing too.

GAME AWARENESS

The same moment means different things in different games.

ShadyCut uses game-specific knowledge to understand terminology, mechanics, locations, items, characters, progression and events. This helps it interpret what the streamer is talking about and what the viewer is actually seeing.

  • “TV Station” → an Arena Breakout map
  • “Character Pull” → a Genshin character acquisition
  • “Launch Site Key” → a Rust monument access item
  • “Knock” → downing another player

02 — SEE THE BIGGER PICTURE

We build a complete view of what happened.

ShadyCut looks across the entire session instead of judging moments in isolation. It connects what the streamer says with what happens in the game, tracks how the session develops over time, and builds a bird’s-eye view of the important activity, changes, decisions, and context.

At this stage, it is not trying to force everything into a story. The goal is simpler: understand the full session well enough that the story can be found from the whole picture, not guessed from a handful of exciting moments.

03 — FIND THE STORY

We figure out what the session is really about.

Once the whole session is understood, ShadyCut looks for the narrative inside it. It identifies the goals, problems, decisions, setbacks, turning points, and payoffs that connect separate moments into something worth watching.

It works out what the viewer needs to see for that story to make sense — what establishes the situation, what changes it, what needs context, and what eventually pays off.

  • Goals and problems
  • Setup and necessary context
  • Decisions and turning points
  • Escalation and setbacks
  • Payoffs and resolutions
  • Must-see moments
  • Supporting moments

STORY-FIRST EDITING

Keep what makes the story work.

Not every exciting moment belongs in the final cut, and not every quiet moment should disappear. ShadyCut evaluates scenes by the role they play in the story — whether they establish context, move something forward, explain a decision, build toward a payoff, or complete an earlier setup.

The result is a clear story structure that the editing stage can build around.

  • Preserve setup → escalation → payoff
  • Keep context before it becomes necessary
  • Remove moments that add nothing to the story
  • Protect important callbacks and resolutions
  • Separate essential moments from supporting material

04 — EDITABLE TIMELINE

You get an editable first cut, ready to refine.

ShadyCut turns the finished story plan into a structured timeline you can actually work with. The important scenes are already selected and arranged, the supporting context is in place, and editorial guidance stays attached where it matters.

It is not a locked AI-generated video. You can open the cut, review the decisions, move scenes, tighten pacing, restyle it, or change the direction entirely.

  • Premiere-ready editable timeline
  • Selected and arranged source clips
  • Story structure already built in
  • Scene notes and editorial context
  • MUST KEEP and REVIEW guidance
  • Easy to review, change and finish
  • Built to save editing time, not replace the editor
