Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

ShadyCut respects your privacy and only collects information that is reasonably needed to operate, improve, and provide the service.

Information you provide

If you apply for the ShadyCut private beta, contact us, join a community space, or otherwise interact with ShadyCut, you may provide information such as your name, email address, creator or social profile links, and information about your content or workflow.

Content submitted to ShadyCut

During testing or use of ShadyCut, creators may provide streams, videos, audio, project files, or related material for processing. This content is used to provide and improve the requested ShadyCut workflow and is not treated as ShadyCut-owned content.

Usage and technical information

ShadyCut may collect basic technical and usage information needed to understand how the website and service are used, maintain reliability, diagnose problems, and improve the product. This may include browser, device, page-view, and interaction information.

How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • provide and operate ShadyCut;
  • process private beta applications;
  • communicate with creators;
  • improve product quality and reliability;
  • understand how the website and product are used;
  • protect the service from abuse or security issues.

Third-party services

ShadyCut may rely on third-party hosting, analytics, infrastructure, communication, or AI service providers where necessary to operate the website and product. These providers may process limited information on ShadyCut’s behalf.

Data sharing

ShadyCut does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers when needed to operate the product, comply with legal obligations, or protect ShadyCut and its users.

Data retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, unless a longer retention period is required for legal or operational reasons.

Your choices

You may contact ShadyCut to ask questions about your information or request that personal information you provided be corrected or deleted, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as ShadyCut develops and new features or services are introduced. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact ShadyCut through the ShadyCut Discord community.
