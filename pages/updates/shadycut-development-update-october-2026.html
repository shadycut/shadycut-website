UPDATE

ShadyCut Development Update — October 2026

A lot has changed under the hood recently. The main focus has been making ShadyCut understand long gaming sessions as actual stories — not just loud moments or random highlights.

Published
Oct 16, 2026
Reading time
—

New Games & Game Understanding

Outlast

  • Added a full Outlast game profile and recognition baseline.
  • Reworked the game-specific Director contract after testing against a real stream of roughly four hours.
  • Horror-specific elements are now explicitly treated as story-critical: scares, threats, chases, hiding, captures, antagonist encounters, setbacks and payoffs.
  • Used real creator feedback to identify where important setup was being lost before major moments.

Arena Breakout Infinite

  • Built a new ABI profile from a real recording of more than three hours.
  • The system can currently distinguish active raids, lobby/preparation, result screens, and simulated replay/spectating.
  • It reconstructed 10 separate raid attempts from the recording without manually marking them.
  • Full-recording recognition was tested locally before moving forward.

Understanding Layer

This is probably the biggest change. The Chapters stage was rebuilt to create a much richer chronological understanding of the entire session.

Instead of mainly looking for isolated moments, it now produces:

  • a session overview,
  • key developments,
  • objectives and outcomes,
  • the streamer arc,
  • mood changes,
  • chronological chapters,
  • detailed scenes inside each chapter,
  • and confidence and evidence references.

The goal is simple:

Understand what actually happened in the session before trying to edit it.

Additional work in this layer includes:

  • improved evidence validation and recovery,
  • improved speaker attribution,
  • richer Evidence Timeline provenance,
  • and a lightweight 540p analysis proxy that speeds up some multi-hour visual analysis runs.

Director Layer

The Director turns the understood session into an actual proposed video.

Recent real-world tests exposed an important failure mode: the system could preserve a payoff while cutting too much of the struggle that made that payoff meaningful.

The general Director rules now better preserve:

setup → escalation → decisions / failed attempts → payoff

Repetition is also no longer treated as automatically redundant when the situation, pressure, decisions or progress are changing.

The responsibilities are now more clearly separated:

  • Global Director Contract: universal storytelling and editing rules.
  • Game-Specific Director Contract: what is story-critical in that particular game.

This lets us teach Outlast what makes a horror story work without pushing horror-specific behavior into every other game.

Output & Editing Layer

The generated edit workflow has also been improved:

  • improved editable timeline/export workflow,
  • cleaner overlay/graphics handling,
  • simplified audio handling for cleaner imports,
  • improved source-media handling for problematic recordings/codecs,
  • safer backups when regenerating expensive AI artifacts,
  • and improved speech-safe cut boundaries after Director selection.

The goal remains the same: a strong first cut that can be opened, reviewed and continued in a real editor, rather than a finished AI video locked in a black box.

The recent work has been focused on getting ShadyCut closer to real-world use: testing it on different games, improving how it understands long sessions, and tightening the path from raw footage to an editable first cut.

There is still plenty to improve, but the system is getting much closer to the thing it was originally meant to become.
